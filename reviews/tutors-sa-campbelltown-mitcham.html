Tutors SA

All subjects 📍 Campbelltown & Mitcham · Reviewed 6 July 2026
4.5 out of 5

Who they are

Tutors SA has been operating since 2013 and reports having supported more than 6500 students in that time. They run two centres, one at 27 Montacute Road in Campbelltown and one on Kyre Avenue in Kingswood for the Mitcham area, covering the eastern and southern sides of the city.

They are a proud member of the Australian Tutoring Association, which is a small detail worth noticing. Industry body membership is voluntary and brings a code of conduct with it, so it is a reasonable signal of a business that takes its professional standards seriously.

What they offer

The subject list is the broadest on this site: Mathematics, English, General Science, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Accounting, Psychology, Legal Studies, Nutrition, Information Technology, Research Project and UCAT, spanning Reception through to Year 12. Accounting, Legal Studies and Research Project in particular are subjects many agencies simply do not staff.

Three delivery formats are on offer, covering small group tutoring, one-on-one tutoring and a "Hybrid - In Class & Online Tutoring" model. Fees are set out in a published schedule of charges that families can download, which is refreshingly transparent for the sector.

What stands out

The tutor credentials are the strongest published on this site. Tutors SA states that "Most of our Adelaide tutors possess postgraduate qualifications, including PhDs and master's degrees", and that English tutors are "registered school teachers with several years of experience". Registered teachers for English is exactly what you want, because marking SACE English well is a trained skill.

They also run dedicated preparation for ACER, Ignite and STEM scholarship exams and report more than 330 scholarships won by their students. If you are targeting a scholarship place, that track record is hard to ignore. Their teaching philosophy centres on "developing flexibility in thinking and reasoning" rather than rote drilling.

Highlights

  • Tutors mostly hold postgraduate qualifications including PhDs and masters degrees
  • English taught by registered school teachers
  • Broadest subject list here, including Accounting, Legal Studies and Research Project
  • More than 330 ACER, Ignite and STEM scholarships reported
  • Member of the Australian Tutoring Association with a published fee schedule

Good to know

  • Two centres, so the eastern and southern suburbs are best served
  • Fees live in a downloadable schedule rather than on the homepage
  • Scholarship preparation is seasonal, so ask about timing early
  • Hybrid in-class and online delivery is worth asking about if travel is tricky
4.5out of 5

The verdict

The most credentialed and broadest option on this site, and the clear first stop for scholarship exam preparation. The combination of postgraduate tutors, registered English teachers and a published fee schedule makes it easy to recommend.
