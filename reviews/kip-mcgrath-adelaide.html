Kip McGrath Education Centres

Primary 📍 Highgate, Salisbury & Morphett Vale · Reviewed 25 May 2026
4.5 out of 5

Who they are

Kip McGrath is a long-established name in Australian tutoring, and the Adelaide region has centres at Highgate, Salisbury and Morphett Vale, spread neatly across the inner south, the north and the far south. Each centre is locally run while following the same national programme.

The focus is deliberately narrow, covering English and maths tutoring for primary and secondary school age children. For families whose child is behind in reading or number work, that narrowness is precisely the point.

What they offer

Every student starts with a free learning assessment, where "a qualified teacher will take your child through age-appropriate activities to identify your child's strengths, as well as any weaknesses, in English or maths". Kip McGrath describes the result as "a true snapshot of your child's current strengths as well as any learning gaps".

From there, sessions run for 60 minutes a week, either in centre in small groups or online using a "digital whiteboard in a safe and controlled environment". The programme is then tailored to what the assessment found.

What stands out

The methodology is systematic in a way that suits struggling learners. Kip McGrath describes an approach where "the student works through each topic area until they have caught up to their year level". Mastery before progression is the right sequence for a child with genuine gaps, because moving on before a topic is solid is exactly how gaps compound in the first place.

Having a qualified teacher run the initial assessment is another strong point. Diagnosis is the hardest part of remediation, and a free, teacher-led assessment before any money changes hands is a fair and confident way to start. Parents receive regular progress reports throughout.

Highlights

  • Free initial assessment conducted by a qualified teacher
  • Systematic approach that works topic by topic until the child catches up
  • Three Adelaide centres covering the inner south, north and far south
  • Regular progress reports to parents
  • Online option uses a supervised digital whiteboard

Good to know

  • English and maths only, so no science or humanities support
  • Sessions are a standard 60 minutes weekly rather than flexible blocks
  • Centres are individually run, so ring your local one for pricing and times
  • Best suited to catching up rather than extension for already strong students
4.5out of 5

The verdict

A dependable choice for primary students who have fallen behind in reading or maths. The free teacher-led assessment and the catch-up-before-you-progress method are exactly right for closing genuine learning gaps.
