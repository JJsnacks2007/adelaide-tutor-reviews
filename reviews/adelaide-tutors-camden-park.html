Adelaide Tutors

Maths 📍 Camden Park · Reviewed 15 June 2026
4.5 out of 5

Who they are

Adelaide Tutors runs from 400 Anzac Highway in Camden Park and does one thing deliberately well: senior maths and physics, taught in small groups. The subject list is short and unapologetic, covering Mathematical Methods, Specialist Mathematics, Physics and Years 8 to 10 Mathematics.

That narrowness is a strength. A practice that teaches only Methods, Specialist and Physics builds far deeper familiarity with those subject outlines than a generalist agency juggling fifteen subjects ever could.

What they offer

Classes are capped at "Small groups of no more than 6", which sits in a genuine sweet spot. Six students is small enough that a tutor can see every worked solution on the desk, but large enough that a good question from one student benefits the whole room and the price stays well below one-to-one rates.

The teaching model is described as "Structured lessons, exam-style practice, individual feedback and small classes". Those four elements in combination are exactly what senior maths students need: content delivered in order, practice under exam conditions and someone actually reading their working.

What stands out

The exam focus is explicit rather than incidental. For Mathematical Methods and Specialist Mathematics in particular, the gap between understanding a concept and executing it under time pressure is where marks are won and lost, and a practice built around exam-style practice with individual feedback is aimed squarely at that gap.

The Camden Park location also serves the western and southwestern suburbs well, an area with fewer senior maths specialists than the eastern side of the city.

Highlights

  • Genuine specialisation in Methods, Specialist Maths and Physics
  • Classes capped at six students
  • Structured lessons paired with exam-style practice and individual feedback
  • Convenient for western and southwestern suburbs families

Good to know

  • Maths and physics only, so you will need someone else for English or humanities
  • In person at Camden Park, with no online stream advertised
  • Rates are not published online, so ring or email to confirm
  • Small class caps mean popular timeslots are worth booking early
4.5out of 5

The verdict

A focused, exam-oriented option for senior maths and physics. If your student is doing Methods or Specialist and lives on the western side of Adelaide, the six-student cap and exam-style practice are well worth the trip to Camden Park.
