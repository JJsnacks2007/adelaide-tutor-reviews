Alchemy Tuition

English 📍 All Adelaide suburbs · Reviewed 21 July 2026
4.5 out of 5

Who they are

Alchemy Tuition sends tutors to your kitchen table. They cover Reception to Year 12 across English, Maths, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Science, History, Geography and Economics, all aligned to the South Australian curriculum, and they serve all Adelaide suburbs with tutors described as local to the area.

For families juggling work and after-school logistics, the in-home model removes a real barrier. No drive across town at 4pm and no sitting in a car park for an hour.

What they offer

Two formats are available. Online lessons use "video chat, a shared whiteboard and interactive features" at $59 an hour, and in-home sessions run at $89 an hour. Both are one-to-one with "Undivided one on one attention" and lessons customised to the student.

Scheduling is unusually generous, running seven days a week from 7am to 8pm, and the whole thing operates on a pay-as-you-go basis with no contracts and no minimum commitment. For families who want to try tutoring without locking into a term, that flexibility is worth a lot.

What stands out

The tutor vetting is more thorough than most. Tutors are "hand selected, interviewed one-on-one and double reference checked", and every tutor holds a Working with Children Check that Alchemy says it "manually verifies" ahead of the first session. Manual verification rather than a box ticked on a form is a meaningful distinction.

Parent reporting is also strong: progress updates arrive after each lesson with detailed reports every ten lessons. On top of that sits a "100% happiness guarantee" on the first lesson, marketed as "Love your tutor (or it's free)". The company reports a five star rating across more than 5000 verified reviews.

Highlights

  • Tutors hand selected, interviewed and double reference checked
  • Working with Children Checks manually verified before the first lesson
  • Progress update after every lesson plus a detailed report every ten
  • Free first lesson if you are not happy with the tutor match
  • Pay as you go with no contracts, seven days a week

Good to know

  • In-home convenience carries a premium at $89 an hour versus $59 online
  • One-to-one only, so there is no cheaper group option
  • Tutor quality will vary by individual, which is what the first-lesson guarantee is for
  • Worth confirming travel coverage for outer suburbs when you book
4.5out of 5

The verdict

The most convenient option here for busy families, with vetting and parent reporting that are a cut above. The first-lesson guarantee makes trying them essentially risk free, and the online rate keeps it affordable if you can skip the home visit.
