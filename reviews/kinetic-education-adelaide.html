Kinetic Education

Maths 📍 Online, Adelaide wide · Reviewed 11 May 2026
4.0 out of 5

Who they are

Kinetic Education brings "30+ Years of tutoring success" to online maths and English tutoring for Years 7 to 12, with content mapped to both the Australian National Curriculum and SACE. The company reports having helped more than 100,000 Australian families and holds a 96 per cent customer satisfaction rating.

Delivery is entirely online, and as they put it, "as long as your child has access to a reliable internet connection, they are good to go". For families in outer suburbs, regional South Australia or simply without a spare afternoon for driving, that removes the logistics problem completely.

What they offer

The model combines a self-paced learning platform with live tutor support. Maths runs on their MATHS DOCTOR platform and English on English Wiz, with a personalised assessment placing each student at the right starting point and a learning plan built from there.

On top of the platform sits a weekly one-on-one session with a tutor via screen share. Tutors are trained in-house and based in Australia, and parents receive weekly tutor feedback.

What stands out

The price is the headline and it is not close. At "From $29/w", Kinetic sits at a fraction of typical one-to-one hourly rates, and new families are offered two free weeks of tutoring to start. For households where $60 to $90 an hour simply is not possible, this changes tutoring from unaffordable to achievable.

The hybrid structure is also well judged. Platform work between sessions means the student is practising regularly rather than only during a weekly hour, while the live session keeps a human being accountable for progress. Weekly feedback to parents closes the loop nicely.

Highlights

  • Outstanding value starting from $29 a week
  • Two free weeks of tutoring for new students
  • Content aligned to both the Australian Curriculum and SACE
  • Weekly one-to-one session on top of self-paced platform work
  • Weekly tutor feedback to parents

Good to know

  • Maths and English only for the high school programme
  • Fully online, so families wanting face-to-face should look elsewhere
  • Works best for students who will engage with platform work between sessions
  • Reliable internet and a quiet workspace at home are assumed
4.0out of 5

The verdict

By far the best value on this site, and a genuinely sensible structure behind the low price. If cost or travel has been the thing stopping you from getting help, the two free weeks make this the easiest place to start.
