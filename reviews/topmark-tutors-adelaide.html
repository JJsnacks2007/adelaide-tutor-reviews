TopMark Tutors

Science 📍 Adelaide · Reviewed 4 August 2026
5.0 out of 5

Who they are

TopMark Tutors was founded by Ahmed Aly and Ali Alebadi, two Flinders University medical students who came through SACE themselves with ATARs of 99.20 and 99.40. That matters more than it might sound. Both founders sat the same exams their students are sitting, in the same state, recently enough that the subject outlines have barely moved.

Ahmed teaches Biology, Physics, General Mathematics, Mathematical Methods, UCAT and Business Innovation, and oversees student outcomes across the practice. Ali covers Physics, Chemistry, Mathematical Methods, General Mathematics and medicine and dentistry preparation. Between them they cover almost the entire SACE STEM load, which is unusual for a practice this focused.

What they offer

There are three pathways rather than one generic hourly rate. Private Tutoring is described as "One-on-one tutoring built entirely around one student: their subjects, their gaps, their pace and their goals". Subject Mastery pairs group classes built on TopMark's own textbooks with a weekly one-on-one session, which is a genuinely smart structure: the group class carries the content while the private session catches whatever the student personally missed.

The third pathway, Academic Mentorship, provides "A personal mentor for goals, accountability, study skills, confidence and direction". Crash courses and dedicated UCAT and medicine and dentistry preparation round out the offering. Everything starts with a free consultation rather than an upfront commitment.

What stands out

The results TopMark publishes are the headline. Every 2025 TopMark student scored a 90 or higher ATAR, the top student finished on 99.80, and the cohort collected three SACE merits alongside seven Physics A+ grades, five Chemistry A+ grades and five Mathematical Methods A or A+ grades. For a practice of this size that is a remarkable strike rate.

Just as telling is how they describe their own values: "Clarity over confusion, Structure over cramming, Confidence over stress, Trust over pressure selling". The stated mission is to "Make tutoring easier to trust and easier to start", and the free consultation and published phone number back that up. Writing their own textbooks rather than photocopying past papers also signals a practice that is building something durable.

Highlights

  • Founded and taught by two Flinders medical students with 99+ ATARs
  • Every 2025 student scored a 90 or higher ATAR, topping out at 99.80
  • Purpose-written TopMark textbooks underpin the group classes
  • Mentorship stream covers study skills and confidence, not just content

Good to know

  • Focused on senior secondary, so the best fit is Years 11 and 12
  • Rates are quoted after the free consultation rather than listed online
  • UCAT and medicine or dentistry preparation runs as its own stream
  • Enquiries go through 0450 113 766 or the online consultation booking

The verdict

The standout SACE specialist on this site. If your student is aiming high in Years 11 and 12 sciences or maths, the published results, purpose-built materials and mentoring make the free consultation an easy first call.
