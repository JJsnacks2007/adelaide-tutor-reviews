About this site

Finding a good tutor in Adelaide shouldn't be guesswork.

Adelaide Tutor Reviews exists because choosing a tutor usually comes down to a Google star rating and a hopeful phone call. I wanted something better for local families, so I started writing it.

Who's behind this

I'm a local with a long-standing interest in education, and I've spent years helping family and friends work out which tutoring service actually suits their child. After one too many conversations that started with "do you know anyone good for Year 11 maths?", I decided to start documenting what I found properly.

This site is a personal project. I'm not affiliated with any tutoring business, school or agency, and no company has ever paid to appear here or to look better than they deserve.

How I review

Every write-up on this site is a desk review. I read everything a company publishes about itself, including its subject list, program structure, tutor credentials, session formats, pricing, guarantees and any results it reports, then I compare that against what other providers in Adelaide offer. Where a company states a fact about itself, I say so and attribute it, and each review links back to the source so you can check it yourself.

Teaching approach

Is there a real method behind the sessions? Diagnosis, structure, materials and how content is sequenced.

Subject coverage

Which subjects and year levels are genuinely supported, and how well they match South Australian syllabuses.

Parent communication

What parents are told and how often. Progress reports, feedback after sessions and transparency about fees.

Value & accessibility

Price measured against what's delivered, plus location, formats offered and how easy it is to get started.

Each category is scored out of five, and the overall rating is my judgement call rather than a strict average, because a provider that does one thing exceptionally well can be a better choice than one that is merely adequate at everything.

What the stars mean

A 5 means the offering is exceptional in its category and I'd put it at the top of a shortlist. A 4 is a strong choice with a specific best-fit student in mind. A 3 means it depends on your child, so read the review closely. Every provider covered here is one I'd be comfortable recommending to someone, with the review explaining who it suits best.

What this site is not

These are researched overviews, not accounts of lessons I have personally attended. I don't collect or publish private feedback about individual tutors, and nothing here should be read as a claim about how any particular student's sessions went. Treat the reviews as a shortlisting tool, then judge the tutor yourself at a trial lesson or consultation.

Independence, corrections and fairness

I don't accept payment, free lessons or gifts from tutoring companies, and there are no affiliate links on this site. Providers are welcome to respond to their review. If I've got something factually wrong or your program details have changed, tell me and I'll correct it and note the correction. Opinions, though, stay mine.

A note for tutoring companies: if you'd like your service considered for review, or you want to correct or update an existing one, get in touch via the contact page. Being reviewed is free and cannot be bought.
